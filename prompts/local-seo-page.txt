WRITING RULES — follow all of these exactly:

1. Write in natural, conversational UK English throughout
2. Short paragraphs only — 2 to 3 sentences maximum per paragraph
3. Mix short and long sentences within each section for readability
4. Never open a sentence with "We offer", "We provide" or "Our team provides"
5. Avoid repeating phrases, sentence structures or ideas across sections
6. Each section must introduce new information — never summarise a previous section
7. Use problem-first writing: name the problem, explain its impact, then present the solution
8. Ground local relevance in the area data provided — use business types, character and context naturally
9. Mention 1 to 2 nearby areas within the content — weave them in naturally, do not list them all
10. Total word count across all fields combined: 900 to 1,200 words
11. Do not use markdown — plain text only inside all string values

SECTION-BY-SECTION INSTRUCTIONS:

hero_title:
- Format: "[Service] [Area]" or "[Service] in [Area], [City]"
- Strong, clear WordPress page title optimised for the primary keyword
- Must contain both the service name and the area

hero_subtitle (Quick Answer — 60 to 90 words exactly):
- This is an AI citation target — write it as a direct, factual answer to the implied search "What does [service] in [area] do?"
- Begin with "[Service] in [area]..." and stay factual and useful
- Cover: what the service delivers, who it helps, why it matters locally
- No taglines, no sales language — just a clear and genuinely useful answer
- Must be 60 to 90 words — count carefully

intro (Problem-first local introduction — 3 paragraphs):
- Opening style: {{intro_style}}
- Paragraph 1: Open with the real problem a local business owner in {{area}} is facing — draw from the pain_points data and the local character. Do NOT start with the brand name.
- Paragraph 2: Explain the impact of that problem in this specific area — use the business types and local character to make it concrete. Reference one nearby area or a local landmark naturally.
- Paragraph 3: Introduce the service as the solution — what it changes and why it works here. Keep it confident, not salesy.

local_section (Local context — 2 paragraphs):
- Do NOT repeat anything already stated in the intro
- Paragraph 1: Describe the specific business and customer landscape in {{area}} and how {{service_name}} fits their situation. Use area_business_types and area_local_character data.
- Paragraph 2: Include a realistic local customer scenario using this pattern: "If you're based in {{area}} or nearby [choose one from nearby_areas]..." — describe what a typical customer experiences before and after using this service.

benefits (Service breakdown — exactly 6 bullets):
- Each bullet must be a practical outcome, not a vague feature
- Format each as: [Short outcome label] — [one-sentence explanation of why it matters]
- Example format: "Mobile-first design — built so visitors can call or book from any phone without frustration"
- Cover distinct aspects: design quality, speed, conversion, SEO, usability, support or reliability
- No two bullets should overlap in meaning
- Do not use "we" in any bullet

why_choose_us (Trust, process and proof — 3 paragraphs):
- Do NOT repeat any benefit already listed in the benefits array
- Paragraph 1: Explain the process — how this service is delivered in 2 to 3 concrete steps. Make it feel transparent and reassuring.
- Paragraph 2: Establish trust through local knowledge, reliability and experience. Use specific language about working with businesses like the ones in {{area}}. Avoid fake stats.
- Paragraph 3: End with an outcomes-focused statement — what clients experience after working with this team. Use phrases like "businesses like yours find that..." or "you'll notice..." — not invented numbers.

faq (Exactly 3 FAQ items):
- FAQ 1 — AI extraction question: question must be "What Does {{service_name}} in {{area}} Do?" or a natural variant. Answer must be 60 to 80 words, factual, and use different wording from hero_subtitle.
- FAQ 2 — Draw from these topics: {{faq_topics}}. Pick the most relevant one. Answer in 40 to 60 words with a specific, practical response. Avoid generic answers.
- FAQ 3 — A trust or process question (e.g. how long it takes, who it's best for, what happens after enquiry). Answer in 40 to 60 words.

cta_heading:
- CTA style: {{cta_style}}
- A strong closing headline that creates mild urgency or invites the next step
- Must reference the service or the area — make it feel specific
- Do NOT start with "Ready to..."

cta (Final closing copy — 2 to 3 sentences only):
- Reduce friction: tell the reader exactly what happens when they get in touch
- Match the brand CTA: {{brand_cta}}
- Offer one clear next step (audit, quote, review, call, consultation)
- Do not repeat the cta_heading
- End with a direct, action-oriented sentence
